## World Gold Council
Gold Return Attribution Model (GRAM):  
https://www.gold.org/goldhub/tools/gold-return-attribution-model

Gold Mid-Year Outlook 2026:  
https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-mid-year-outlook-2026

GRAM motivates four broad global themes:
- economic expansion
- risk & uncertainty
- opportunity cost (FX/rates)
- momentum
